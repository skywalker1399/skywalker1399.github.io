PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Professional Self-Assessment

A reflection on my Computer Science education, technical strengths, professional growth, and preparation for a career in software development.

Read Full Self-Assessment+

Throughout my experience in the Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University, I have developed the technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities, and professional skills necessary to pursue a career in software development. My coursework has introduced me to several areas of computer science, including software engineering, algorithms and data structures, database management, cybersecurity, and full-stack application development. These experiences have helped me understand not only how to create functional software but also how to design applications that are efficient, maintainable, and secure. Developing my ePortfolio has allowed me to reflect on my progress, recognize my strengths, and demonstrate how I have applied the knowledge gained throughout the program.

One of the most important lessons I have learned is the value of collaboration and communication in software development. Creating successful software often requires developers to work with others, consider different perspectives, and communicate technical information clearly. Throughout my coursework, I have gained experience documenting programming decisions, evaluating software solutions, and incorporating feedback to improve my work. These experiences have helped me recognize the importance of maintaining readable code and clear documentation so that other developers can understand, maintain, and expand an application. I have also developed a greater appreciation for communicating with stakeholders who may not have a technical background. Being able to explain an application's functionality, identify potential concerns, and justify development decisions is essential when creating software that meets organizational and user needs.

My understanding of data structures and algorithms has also grown significantly throughout the program. I have gained experience working with arrays, vectors, hash tables, linked lists, and graphs, while learning how different data structures influence the performance and functionality of applications. Beyond traditional programming assignments, I explored algorithmic problem-solving through artificial intelligence coursework involving reinforcement learning. Working with techniques such as Q-learning and deep reinforcement learning introduced me to algorithms that make decisions based on rewards, experience, and changing environments. These assignments strengthened my analytical thinking and demonstrated how algorithmic principles can be applied to problems beyond conventional software applications.

Software engineering has become one of my strongest areas of interest throughout the program. I have developed applications using languages and technologies such as C++, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, and SQL. One particularly valuable experience involved developing a full-stack travel application using the MEAN stack, which consists of MongoDB, Express, Angular, and Node.js. This project introduced me to the relationship between frontend interfaces, backend services, APIs, and database operations. I implemented functionality for retrieving, adding, and modifying travel information while learning how different components communicate within a web application. Working through challenges involving API requests, authentication, and frontend integration strengthened my debugging skills and helped me develop a better understanding of modern software architecture.

Database management has been another important area of development. Throughout the program, I learned how applications use databases to organize, retrieve, and maintain information. My experience with MongoDB introduced me to document-oriented database structures and the use of Mongoose for managing application data. I also developed experience with relational database concepts through SQLite and SQL operations. Working with different database technologies helped me understand that database selection and design depend on an application's requirements. These experiences reinforced the importance of data organization, maintaining consistency, and developing reliable methods for accessing and modifying stored information.

Security has become an increasingly important part of my approach to software development. Through coursework involving secure coding practices, vulnerability analysis, and reverse engineering, I developed a better understanding of how software weaknesses can affect users and organizations. I gained experience examining assembly code, interpreting program behavior, and identifying potential security vulnerabilities in existing applications. I also explored security practices in web development by implementing JSON Web Token authentication and protected API routes within my full-stack application. These experiences taught me the importance of validating input, controlling access to sensitive functionality, and considering security throughout the software development lifecycle rather than treating it as an afterthought.

Completing the Computer Science program has also helped me develop stronger problem-solving and independent learning skills. Many of my programming assignments involved challenges that required careful debugging, research, testing, and evaluation. Whether resolving errors in C++ applications, troubleshooting database connections, or correcting authentication issues in web applications, I learned to approach problems systematically rather than relying on trial and error. These experiences strengthened my confidence in my ability to work with unfamiliar technologies and adapt to new development environments.

Developing my ePortfolio has provided an opportunity to bring these skills together and demonstrate my growth throughout the program. Revisiting earlier coursework allowed me to evaluate my previous programming decisions from a more experienced perspective and identify opportunities for improvement. My three capstone enhancements demonstrate my ability to apply software engineering principles, algorithmic problem-solving, and database management techniques to existing applications. More importantly, the process of enhancing these projects reinforced the value of testing, documentation, maintainability, and secure development practices.

As I prepare to enter the next stage of my professional career, my goal is to continue growing as a software developer and contribute to the creation of reliable, efficient, and secure applications. I value continuous learning, attention to detail, and the ability to develop practical solutions to complex problems. The Computer Science program has provided me with a strong technical foundation, while the capstone experience has helped me demonstrate how that knowledge can be applied to real software development challenges.

This ePortfolio represents my development as a computer science professional and highlights the skills, knowledge, and experience I have gained throughout my education. It demonstrates my ability to analyze problems, develop solutions, work with different programming technologies, and continually improve the quality of my work. I believe these abilities have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to a professional software development environment while continuing to expand my knowledge and experience in the field.
